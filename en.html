Technology

What each technology does and what you feel.

The question I get the most is “does it hurt?”. Instead of just saying no, I’d rather tell you exactly what happens — and remind you that at any moment you can say so and I’ll adjust.

EMShape Neo

HIFEM technology · muscle contraction
What it is
A muscle toning machine that works three areas in one session: abdomen, back and glutes.
How it works
A high-intensity electromagnetic field triggers involuntary muscle contractions — deeper than the ones you can produce on your own, because they don’t depend on your effort.
What it’s used for
Toning and definition of abdomen and glutes. Popular with people who already train and want to show what they’ve built.
What you feel
The contraction feels strong and strange the first time, but it isn’t pain. You lie down and the intensity rises gradually — if it bothers you, say so and I turn it down right away.
What studies show

In a 22-patient study followed by MRI, four HIFEM abdominal sessions showed an average 15% increase in rectus abdominis thickness and an average 18% reduction in fat in the area.

Kinney & Lozanova, Lasers in Surgery and Medicine, 2019 — study conducted with Emsculpt (BTL) equipment.

Ultrasound cavitation

Ultrasound technology
How it works
Low-frequency ultrasound waves applied to the treated area.
What it’s used for
Localized fat and measurements in specific spots — belly, flanks, outer thighs.
What you feel
You’ll hear a high-pitched tone during the session. That’s normal and not a fault: it’s the ultrasound frequency reaching your ear through bone. On the skin it feels like a warm device gliding.
What studies show

Literature reviews describe ultrasound cavitation as a safe procedure for reducing localized measurements, with reported abdominal circumference reductions in the 2 to 4.6 cm range over a series of sessions.

Radiofrequency

Controlled heating technology
How it works
Radiofrequency waves heat the deeper layers of the skin in a controlled way, with no cutting and no needles.
What it’s used for
Skin firmness and texture. It’s the technology most often used in combination with the others, rather than alone.
What you feel
A gentle warmth that builds up, like a warm stone moving over you. I track the temperature the whole time — if it gets too warm, just say so.
What studies show

Body-contouring literature studies radiofrequency mainly in combination with other technologies rather than in isolation — which is how it is normally used in practice.

Who does it

A device is a tool. What decides the result is whose hands are on you.

Every session is run by me, start to finish. No rotation, no assistants — the same person who assessed you is the one working on you.

Which device makes sense for you — and which doesn’t — is what we decide together at the assessment. It depends on your body and your history, and that can’t be answered over a message.

The studies above were run with specific devices and on groups of people. They help you understand what the technology does — they don’t predict your result, which depends on your body, your history and consistency.
